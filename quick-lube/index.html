Do I need an appointment?

No appointment for the lube bay; just drive in during posted hours. Detailing is the only service that books ahead. Every oil change comes with a free exterior car wash, so you roll out with the maintenance done and the car clean. Prices are starting-at and exclude tax.

Can I combine an oil change with a wash?

That is the whole point of the stop. Every oil change includes a free exterior car wash, and the wash, detail center and lube bay sit at the same address, so one visit covers all three. Show the coupon on our home page and take $5 off any oil change.
