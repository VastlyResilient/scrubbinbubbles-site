A proper wash,every visit

Three exterior packages, a hand towel dry on every one, and interior add-ons when the inside needs it too. Prices do not include tax.

Bronze, $11: exterior wash and towel dry. Wheel Deal, $18: adds poly sealant, rim cleaner, rush inhibitor, tire shine and undercarriage wash. Platinum, $22: everything plus double bond teflon, glaze and sealant waxes, triple foam and Vision Clear. Full interior service adds $18 to any package.

How does the Unlimited Wash Club work?

One monthly price from $22.99, wash as often as you like. Plans are vehicle-specific, one vehicle per plan, and apply to Soft Touch Tunnel washes only, not Touchless or Self-Serve. Membership renews automatically each month on your sign-up day. Prices exclude sales tax.

Do you offer a re-wash if it rains after I leave?

One reviewer wrote that previous owners provided a thorough cleaning and a free re-wash when it rains. Those are the reviewer's words, not a posted policy, so we will not print it as a promise. Ask at either counter and we will tell you exactly where you stand. Really.

Review quoted: a YellowPages reviewer, writing about previous ownership.

Can I add interior cleaning?

Add $18 to any wash package for full service: interior vacuuming, windows cleaned inside and out, door jambs dried, dashboard and center console dusted. The Ultimate Full Service, $65 on top of any package, adds an under-seat blow-out, floor-mat shampoo and dash and door dressing.
