Straight answers aboutyour car care

The questions we hear at the counter, answered the same way we answer them there. Really.

How often should I detail my car?

For daily drivers in Connecticut, twice a year is a workable rhythm: once before winter to seal paint against road salt, once in spring to clean up after it. Express wax between details keeps protection topped up. Packages start at $25 for express services and $150 for full packages.

Is an automatic wash safe for my paint?

Our tunnel is a soft-touch wash run with quality products and current equipment, and every pass ends with a hand towel dry. Wash Club plans apply to the Soft Touch Tunnel only, not Touchless or Self-Serve, so you always know which wash your car is getting.

What is a quick lube service?

A drive-in oil change plus light maintenance while you are already at the wash: oils from $39.99 up to five quarts on most vehicles, transmission and fuel injector services at $99.99, power steering at $79.99, wipers and filters from $9.99. Every oil change includes a free exterior wash.

How does the Unlimited Wash Club work?

One monthly price from $22.99, wash as often as you like in the Soft Touch Tunnel. Plans are vehicle-specific, renew automatically each month on your sign-up day, and exclude sales tax. Cancel by emailing scrubbin1@gmail.com at least seven days before your billing date; there are no refunds.

How do I use a coupon?

Print the coupon or show it on your phone from this site and present it before you pay. One coupon per customer per visit. Current offers take $2 off a Platinum wash, $3 off Platinum Full Service, $5 off Ultimate Full Service, $10 off a Bumper to Bumper detail and $5 off any oil change.

Do hours change with the weather?

All posted hours are weather permitting at both locations. North Colony runs Monday to Saturday 7 AM to 8 PM and Sunday 7 AM to 7 PM; South Colony runs Monday to Saturday 7 AM to 7 PM and Sunday 7 AM to 6 PM. On heavy-rain days, call before you drive over.
