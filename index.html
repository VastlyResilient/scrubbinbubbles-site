What does a Scrubbin' Bubbles wash include?

Every wash starts with the exterior and a hand towel dry; the Bronze Wash covers those two basics for $11. Step up to the Wheel Deal at $18 for sealant, rim cleaner and tire shine, or the Platinum at $22 for the full triple-foam works with undercarriage wash. Add $18 to any package for full interior service.

Full wash menu

Can I get my car detailed while I wait?

Yes, the express menu is built for that. Express wax, interior ultra clean, carpet shampoo, upholstery shampoo and leather conditioning are all while-you-wait services, starting at $25. Full detail packages like the Bumper to Bumper are by appointment, starting at $185.
