About · Wallingford, CT

Forty years onthe same corner

For more than 40 years, Scrubbin' Bubbles has been keeping your car spotless, inside and out, while also making it run smoothly.

Who owns Scrubbin' Bubbles?

A family. Scrubbin' Bubbles has been family owned and operated for over 40 years, washing and detailing cars on Colony Road in Wallingford. You may have read that the business changed hands in recent years; what has not changed is the name, the corners and the standard.

Why the flamingo?

Why not. A car wash should be easy to spot and hard to forget, so the bird is on our sign, our wash-club card and every coupon we print. Ask a local where to get a wash and they describe the flamingo. That is the whole idea, and it has worked for decades.

What has not changed in 40 years?

The promise: keep your car spotless, inside and out, while also making it run smoothly. We use the latest technologies and only quality products to give you the same great service you have always been used to. Wash, detailing and quick lube, one stop, same corners.

Want to work at the wash?

We hire for the wash, the detail center and the lube bay at both locations. Email us with your name, which location you prefer, the days you can work, a phone number and any automotive experience. You must be at least 16 and eligible to work in the US.

How does this site handle your information?

Plainly. This site collects nothing behind your back: no accounts, no tracking pixels of our own. If you email us or join the coupon list, we use that address to answer you and send deals. We never sell your information. Fonts load from Google, which sees your browser request.
