Is detailing worth it in winter?

Yes, and Connecticut winters are the argument. Road salt sits on paint and eats at the undercarriage, so an oxidation removal and wax, starting at $150, plus regular undercarriage washes protect the finish through salt season. Spring cleanup goes faster when the paint was sealed in fall.
